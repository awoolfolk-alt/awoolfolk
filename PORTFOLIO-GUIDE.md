### Quest 3.1: Web App Walkthrough · *The Legend of Andre*
This portfolio is also a playable game.

- **Tech stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Phaser 3, Framer Motion
- **The problem it solves:** Recruiters see hundreds of portfolios that all look alike. This one lets them *experience* my story, so they remember it.
- **What I built:**
  - A combat input system that maps keys to actions and buffers inputs so attacks feel responsive
  - A 3-hit basic combo on SPACE (left → right → left) whose damage scales with level (25 / 35 / 45 / 65)
  - A two-part heavy attack on LEFT CLICK: an overhead slam, then an upward launch
  - A dash on SHIFT with after-images, a short invulnerability window, and a cooldown
  - A reusable dialogue system that every NPC and story moment uses
  - Story bosses that unlock new powers when you learn their lessons
